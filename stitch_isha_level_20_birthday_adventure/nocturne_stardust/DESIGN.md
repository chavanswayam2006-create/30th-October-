## Brand & Style

The brand personality merges the intimate, tender sentiment of a private scrapbook with the polished allure of high-end editorial magazines and nostalgic, starlit arcade elements. It tells a celebratory personal narrative: a milestone 20th birthday celebration honoring shared memories, inside jokes, and deeply felt devotion. The tone is deeply romantic, magical, cozy, and reverent without feeling solemn—balancing warm candlelight intimacy with playful arcade metaphors ("Level 20", unlocked chapters, quest logs, and collectible polaroid memories).

The visual aesthetic balances three distinct worlds:
- **Editorial Elegance**: Editorial serif display typography, expansive breathing room, golden hairline dividers, and curated layout pacing reminiscent of bespoke art monographs.
- **Tactile Scrapbook**: Polaroid photo enclosures with gentle organic tilts (-2° to +2°), translucent washi-tape corners, delicate wax-seal stamps, and handwritten script annotations capturing intimate marginalia.
- **Luminous Retro-Arcade**: Glowing stardust nodes, pill-shaped golden milestone badges, subtle pixel-art flourishes framed in champagne gold filigree, and soft ambient bioluminescent glows layered across deep nocturnal fields.

The UI evokes wonder, warmth, nostalgia, and private emotional resonance, transporting the viewer into an exclusive celestial sanctuary crafted specifically for them.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immersive, nocturnal reverie grounded in deep shadows and illuminated by warm luminescence and soft botanical undertones:

- **Primary (`#E8C987` - Champagne Gold)**: The primary luminous accent. Used for headline highlights, focal CTAs, hairline ornamental borders, wax-seal embellishments, active progress nodes, and celebratory level milestones.
- **Secondary (`#F4B6C2` - Blush Rose)**: The emotional, tender voice. Applied to love notes, heart iconography, subtle status accents, decorative script highlights, and gradient transitions against midnight surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#C4B5FD` - Lavender Mist)**: The arcade stardust dimension. Used for celestial glow gradients, secondary badges, quest item borders, and subtle cosmic dust particles across the background canvas.
- **Neutral (`#1B1030` - Deep Midnight Plum)**: The foundational void. Forms the core body background, transitioning into inky navy (`#0F0A1C`) at the canvas base to create an infinite, intimate night sky depth.

### Contextual Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base**: `#1B1030` (Midnight Plum) blended with radial atmospheric vignettes in `#0F0A1C` (Ink Navy).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Translucent Nocturne)**: `rgba(27, 16, 48, 0.65)` layered with backdrop blur for glassy UI enclosures.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Candlelight Cream)**: `#FFF6EA`. Reserved strictly for tactile keepsake surfaces: physical love letters, polaroid frames, and unrolled story scrolls, providing crisp contrast and warm paper tactile warmth.
- **Text & Content**: Primary headings on dark surfaces use `#FFF6EA` or `#E8C987`; body text uses `#E0D7EC` for effortless readability in dark mode. On tactile cream cards (`#FFF6EA`), text shifts to deep sepia plum (`#2B1B3D`) with muted blush accents (`#8E5B6A`).

## Typography

The typography system relies on a curated dialogue between high-fashion editorial presence and clean modern interaction:

- **Display & Headline Voice (`Playfair Display`)**: Conveys timeless elegance and monumental celebration. Used for titles, chapter markers ("Level 20: The Golden Decade"), and intimate section heads. Italicized styling is heavily favored for emotional emphasis, evocative pull quotes, and chapter subtitles.
- **Functional Body & Interface Voice (`DM Sans`)**: Provides crisp, unpretentious legibility for long-form love letters, timeline descriptions, and user interface controls. Ensures readability remains effortless even on small mobile viewports during low-light nighttime reading.
- **Handwritten Accent Layer (`Epilogue` / Script Integration Rule)**: For marginalia, dates scribbled on polaroids, secret side-quests, and Swayam's personal signature, implement a dedicated fluid script layer set in italics with generous line spacing and relaxed tracking.
- **Editorial Micro-Copy**: All `label-sm` and badge headers are set in uppercase with deliberate tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to create a refined arcade-interface HUD feel.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a flexible, narrative-driven grid designed to mimic a high-end luxury digital scrapbook:

- **Container Strategy**: The primary content spine is centered with a max-width of `880px` for reading intimacy, expanding to `1200px` for multi-column gallery spreads and polaroid mosaic stages.
- **Rhythm & Cadence**: Alternating density creates emotional peaks and valleys. Dense scrapbook clusters (overlapping photos, stamps, audio players) are separated by expansive vertical gaps (`space-2xl`) featuring solitary illuminated quotes or golden progress nodes.
- **Breakpoints & Adaptation**:
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins. Complex overlapping polaroid layouts collapse into touch-swipable single-axis carousels or staggered single-column polaroids. Interactive arcade HUD docks stick unobtrusively to the screen bottom.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Polaroid clusters support dual-card side-by-side reveals.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column layout with `4rem` margins. Enables wide editorial spreads, asymmetrical photo cascades with negative space margins, and persistent timeline breadcrumbs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through translucent atmospheric glassmorphism, dual candlelight highlights, and golden ambient backlighting:

- **Glassmorphism Layers**: Frosted glass surfaces (`rgba(27, 16, 48, 0.65)`) utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` paired with an ultra-fine perimeter stroke (`1px solid rgba(232, 201, 135, 0.25)`). This simulates illuminated crystalline HUD panels hovering in deep midnight space.
- **Luminous Ambient Shadowing**: Floating interactive cards avoid harsh murky drop-shadows, using instead a dual-cast atmospheric aura:
  - Base shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 5, 20, 0.7)` for physical elevation.
  - Golden core glow: `0 0 24px 0 rgba(232, 201, 135, 0.12)` providing candlelight diffusion.
- **Tactile Paper Elevation**: Physical letter envelopes and Polaroid frames use `#FFF6EA` surfaces with crisp contact shadows: `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.25), 0 16px 36px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`, grounding them as authentic physical artifacts sitting upon the digital velvet stage.
- **Interactive Phosphor Glow**: Active badges, arcade checkpoint dots, and primary CTA buttons emit an intensified ambient bloom on hover (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(232, 201, 135, 0.45)`).

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, protective geometry balancing structural editorial balance with tactile warmth:

- **Base Radius (`roundedness: 2`)**: Interactive cards, scrapbook windows, and dialogue modals default to a generous `1.5rem` (24px) border radius, conveying coziness, approachability, and organic warmth.
- **Tactile Artifacts**: Polaroid frames preserve classic rectilinear geometry with slightly softened corners (`0.5rem` / 8px) to reflect authentic photographic cardstock.
- **Arcade Controls & Badges**: Progress pills, milestone badges ("Level 20 Cleared!"), audio controls, and interactive pill-buttons use full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px).
- **Washi Tape & Seals**: Decorative washi-tape corners are styled with subtle ragged terminal trims and 15% opacity, while wax seals use organic, asymmetrical circular masks.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Celestial Action**: Golden radiance button. Background: linear gradient (`135deg, #E8C987, #D4AF37`) with dark plum typography (`#1B1030`), weight 600. Features a subtle inner highlight and a continuous gentle ambient glow (`0 0 16px rgba(232, 201, 135, 0.3)`). Scales smoothly to `1.03` on hover with intensified bloom.
- **Secondary Glass Action**: Translucent midnight base (`rgba(27, 16, 48, 0.6)`) bounded by a hairline border (`1px solid rgba(232, 201, 135, 0.4)`), text rendered in `#FFF6EA`. Hover triggers a soft blush tint (`rgba(244, 182, 194, 0.15)`).

### Polaroid Frames & Keepsakes
- Heavy cream borders (`#FFF6EA`) with a 12px top/side margin and a 40px bottom margin for handwritten captions.
- Organic rotational variance: alternating elements automatically apply `-1.5deg` and `+2deg` transforms. Hovering gently straightens the image to `0deg` and elevates the card via z-index transition.
- Upper corners feature semi-translucent washi tape strips (`rgba(244, 182, 194, 0.45)`) positioned at `-45deg`.

### Wax-Sealed Secret Envelopes
- Deep plum cardstock envelope flap locked with an embossed 3D seal in `#E8C987` featuring Swayam's initial or an arcade heart icon.
- Clicking the seal triggers a smooth split animation, unfolding into the full-width candlelight cream (`#FFF6EA`) handwritten letter.

### Arcade Milestones & Level HUD
- **Progress Track**: A champagne gold hairline path connecting glowing milestone nodes (Levels 1 to 20). Unlocked stages emit a pulsing stardust ring (`#E8C987`), while upcoming levels rest in muted lavender (`rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.3)`).
- **Level Badge**: Pill-shaped badge featuring 8-bit champagne heart glyphs alongside modern Playfair typography: `"LEVEL 20 UNLOCKED · OCT 30, 2026"`.

### Form Fields & Interactive Inputs (Trivia / Memory Unlocks)
- Inputs utilize frosted dark glass fields with gold hairline borders.
- Placeholder text in muted lavender mist (`rgba(196, 181, 253, 0.5)`).
- On focus, border transitions to solid `#E8C987` with an ambient champagne outer ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(232, 201, 135, 0.2)`).
